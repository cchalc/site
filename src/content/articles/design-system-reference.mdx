## Text

Body copy is Schibsted Grotesk, **strong text** steps up to `fg-0`, and
[links](https://design.within.website/) invert to cream-on-magenta on hover.
Inline `code` is a small chip, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> is a keyboard
key, and <mark>marked text</mark> uses the bright purple.
