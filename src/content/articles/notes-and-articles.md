Most blogs die of friction. You have a thought, but publishing it means
wrestling with tooling, so the thought stays in a notes app forever.

My fix is to separate the two activities. **Notes** are where thinking
happens — fast, messy, no audience. **Articles** are the small fraction of
notes worth polishing for other people.

The pipeline between them is intentionally short:

1. Write notes while I work, wherever is convenient.
2. When a note is worth sharing, hand it to an agent with the site's
   conventions.
3. Review the draft, tweak the voice, publish.

The site is designed so step 2 is trivial: one Markdown file, a few
frontmatter fields, and a `draft` flag that keeps work-in-progress out of
production until I flip it. Nothing else stands between a good idea and a
published post.
